User manual

  1. Connect your digital piano with MIDI interface to computer — or just use the keyboard drawer at the bottom of the screen
  2. Open this website in a browser that supports Web MIDI — Chrome, Edge or Opera, or a recent Firefox. The line at the right of the keyboard drawer tells you what it found.
  3. Select difficulty level
  4. Play the note you see
  5. Improve yourself!

No piano to hand? Click the on-screen keys, or play with your computer keyboard: A–J for white notes, W/E/T/Y/U for black, Z/X to change octave.
